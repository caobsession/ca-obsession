Data Protection
IMPORTANT NOTICE

Before using CA Obsession seriously, please understand how your data works.

This app stores your study data locally inside your browser on this device. Your data is private, offline, and fully under your control — but it also means you are responsible for protecting it.

Before making major changes, always export a backup first. If something behaves unexpectedly, you can use Reset App Data safely after creating a backup.

📦 Your Data — Key Facts

CA Obsession does not use accounts, cloud storage, or online servers. Everything is stored locally on your device and browser. If you switch devices, clear browser data, or lose local storage, your data can only be recovered from a backup.

🚀 Quick Start — Recommended
  • Enable Persistent Storage
  • Export backups regularly
  • Use one primary browser and device
  • Avoid Incognito / Private Mode
  • Read the User Manual before importing or resetting data

Most data-loss problems can be avoided by following these simple habits.

📤 Export backups regularly

Go to Settings → Export Backup and save outside your browser — Google Drive, email, USB, external storage, or anywhere else on your device but outside the browser storage.

🔒 Enable Persistent Storage

Go to Settings → Storage → Protect to request that your browser preserves local study data.

🚫 Avoid clearing browser data

Clearing cookies, site storage, or browser data may permanently erase your locally stored study data, uploaded attachments, progress records, notes, and tasks if no backup exists.

⚠️ Important Backup Limitation

Backups safely restore your subjects, chapters, lectures, progress, revision data, tasks, notes, settings, study links, text-based content, and most study information.

However, uploaded PDFs, images, presentations, videos, and other file attachments are not included in backup exports.

If browser storage is cleared, the app is reset, or you move to a different device, these uploaded files may no longer be available and may need to be uploaded again.

Before using Reset App Data, always create a fresh backup export.

☁️ Recommended Workflow: Store important study files in cloud storage and save their links inside CA Obsession. Resource links and text-based content are included in backups, making device migration easier while reducing local storage usage inside the application.

For maximum reliability, keep regular backups and use one primary browser whenever possible.
